Contract Specifications
Instrument : MCX Silver Mini Futures Lot Size : 5 KG Multiplier : 5 Quote : ₹ per KG (MCX) · $ per troy oz (XAGUSD) 1 Troy Ounce = 31.1034768 grams 1 KG = 32.1507466 troy ounces
MCX Price from XAGUSD
MCX (₹/KG) = XAGUSD × USDINR × 32.1507466
Derives the fair MCX price from international prices. Add premium/basis as needed.
Reverse XAGUSD from MCX
XAGUSD = Target_MCX ÷ (USDINR × 32.1507466)
Reverse USDINR from MCX
USDINR = Target_MCX ÷ (XAGUSD × 32.1507466)
P&L Calculation
Gross P&L = (Current_MCX − Entry_MCX) × Lot_Size_KG × Number_of_Lots Net P&L = Gross_P&L − Brokerage − Taxes
Percentage Return
P&L % = (Current_MCX − Entry_MCX) ÷ Entry_MCX × 100
Break-even Price
Break-even MCX = Entry_MCX + (Total_Charges ÷ (LotSize × NumLots)) Break-even XAGUSD = Break-even_MCX ÷ (Entry_USDINR × 32.1507466)
With Premium Adjustment
MCX = (XAGUSD × USDINR × 32.1507466) + Premium + Basis
Sensitivity
MCX change per $1 XAGUSD move = USDINR × 32.1507466 MCX change per ₹1 USDINR move = XAGUSD × 32.1507466
